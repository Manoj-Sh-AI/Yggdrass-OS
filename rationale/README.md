# Rationale

Why things are the way they are. One short decision record per non-obvious choice, in `decisions/`.

Copy `decisions/_template.md`. A record for a row in the register of `planning/locked-plan-v1.md` is named by its D-number: `D02-node-identity.md` is D2. Records that predate the register keep their own sequence (`0001-project-setup.md`). Nothing is renumbered. Never delete old records (supersede them instead).

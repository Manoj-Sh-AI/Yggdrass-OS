## Facts

- [stated] Trial ran over three seasons, 2025 to 2027 (src: Okafor 2029 p.12, 2030-02-02)
- [stated] Reports that irrigating at 70 percent of full demand retains most yield (src: Okafor 2029 p.13, 2030-02-02)
- [stated] Average yield loss at 70 percent was 4 percent (src: Okafor 2029 p.14, 2030-02-02)
- [stated] Measures a 30 percent water saving at that level (was 35 percent in the preprint, changed 2030-02-04) (src: erratum, 2030-02-04)
- [stated] Tested only on one region's orchards, not other climates (src: Okafor 2029 p.16, 2030-02-02)
- [derived] The 30 percent saving assumes drip irrigation throughout (src: agent, 2030-02-06)

## Facts

- [stated] Launched 1969-07-16 from Kennedy Space Center Launch Complex 39A (src: NASA Apollo 11 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Lunar module Eagle landed on 1969-07-20 in the Sea of Tranquility (src: NASA Apollo 11 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Splashed down in the Pacific on 1969-07-24 (src: NASA Apollo 11 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Command module was Columbia and lunar module was Eagle (src: NASA Apollo 11 mission page, 2026-10-24)

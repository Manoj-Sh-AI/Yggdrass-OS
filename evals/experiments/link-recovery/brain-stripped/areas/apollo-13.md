## Facts

- [stated] Launched 1970-04-11 (src: NASA Apollo 13 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Mission goal became a safe return to Earth (was a lunar landing at Fra Mauro, changed 1970-04-13 by Mission Control abort decision) (src: NASA Apollo 13 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Command module pilot is Jack Swigert (was Ken Mattingly, changed 1970-04-10 by KSC management meeting) (src: NASA history Apollo 13 measles article, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Crew used the lunar module Aquarius as a lifeboat and splashed down on 1970-04-17 (src: NASA Apollo 13 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] The Fra Mauro landing site was later reached by Apollo 14 in February 1971 (src: NASA history, 2026-10-24)
- [derived] The crew swap and the explosion are unrelated: the swap was decided before launch (src: agent, 2026-10-24)

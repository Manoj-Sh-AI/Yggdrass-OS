## Facts

- [stated] Throttle reduction came at 386 seconds after ignition against 384 predicted, indicating nominal engine performance (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-4, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] The first of five computer alarms came about 5 minutes after descent initiation (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-4, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] High gate and the switch to program P64 came at 7129 feet at 125 feet per second (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-4, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] The approach path led into a large crater, forcing an unplanned redesignation (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-4, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Manual control began at about 600 feet and the craft landed about 1100 feet downrange of the initial aim point (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-5, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] About 95 pounds of reaction control propellant were used against 40 predicted (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-6, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Manual-mode reaction control use was 51 pounds, about 1.5 times the automatic rate (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-6, 2026-10-24)

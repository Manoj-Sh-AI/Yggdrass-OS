## Facts

- [stated] Backup lunar module pilot Charlie Duke caught German measles and exposed the crews (src: NASA history Apollo 13 measles article, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Blood tests from the 1970-04-06 physicals showed Ken Mattingly had no rubella immunity (src: NASA history Apollo 13 measles article, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Flight surgeon Charles Berry worried Mattingly could fall ill alone in the command module in lunar orbit (src: NASA history Apollo 13 measles article, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] The swap was decided at a KSC management meeting and announced at a press conference on 1970-04-10 (src: NASA history Apollo 13 measles article, 2026-10-24)

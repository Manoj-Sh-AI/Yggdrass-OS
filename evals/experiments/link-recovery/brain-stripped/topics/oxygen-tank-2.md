## Facts

- [stated] Exploded on 1970-04-13, about 56 hours into the flight (src: NASA Apollo 13 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Its thermostatic switches were built to a 1962 specification for 28 volts DC (src: Moonport SP-4204, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] The heater specification was revised in 1965 to 65 volts but the switches were not changed (src: Moonport SP-4204, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Before launch it was emptied by running its heaters on 65-volt ground power for about eight hours (src: Moonport SP-4204, 2026-10-24)

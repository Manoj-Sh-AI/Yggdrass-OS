## Facts

- [stated] The command module's lithium hydroxide canisters were square and the lunar module's were round (src: NASA Apollo 13 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Engineers devised an adapter from hoses, cardboard, plastic bags and tape (src: NASA Apollo 13 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] The crew built it from radioed instructions (src: NASA Apollo 13 mission page, 2026-10-24)

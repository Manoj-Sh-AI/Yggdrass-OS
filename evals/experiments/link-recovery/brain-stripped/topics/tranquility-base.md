## Facts

- [stated] Named by Neil Armstrong at touchdown: Houston, Tranquility Base here, the Eagle has landed (src: NASA Apollo 11 mission page, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Actual landing point was 0 degrees 41 minutes 15 seconds north, 23 degrees 26 minutes east (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-6, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] Lies in Mare Tranquillitatis, the Sea of Tranquility (src: NASA Apollo 11 mission page, 2026-10-24)

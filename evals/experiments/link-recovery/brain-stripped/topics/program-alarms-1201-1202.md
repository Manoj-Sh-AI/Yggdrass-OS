## Facts

- [stated] Five computer alarms occurred during the powered descent (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-4, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] The first came about 5 minutes after descent initiation (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-4, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] They did not degrade any primary guidance or control function (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-4, 2026-10-24)
- [stated] They interfered with the crew's early assessment of the landing approach (src: Apollo 11 Mission Report p.5-4, 2026-10-24)

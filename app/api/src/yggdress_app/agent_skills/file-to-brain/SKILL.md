name: file-to-brain
description: Decide what belongs in Manoj's brain (Lia's notes) and offer it for filing under the approval rules.
domains: [brain]
---

The brain holds durable facts about Manoj's life: people, places, projects, goals, decisions,
events, sources and what he learned. It is not a task list, a calendar or a chat log.

File it when it is:
- a fact that will still be true or useful in a month (a new job detail, a person's role,
  a decision and its reason, a date that matters);
- stated by Manoj or by a source he trusts, not guessed.

Do not file:
- passing moods, small talk, one-off logistics ("I'm 5 minutes late");
- anything already in the brain (search first: an alias or near-identical fact means it exists);
- secrets, passwords, card numbers or health details unless Manoj explicitly asks.

Approval rules (never break them):
- Adding a new fact, note or link is offered as a card; it is written at once when Manoj
  approves, with Undo.
- Replacing, removing, unlinking or archiving always shows a preview first and waits for
  Confirm. Say plainly when an action cannot be undone (remove and unlink cannot).
- Never write silently, and never say something was saved before the card is approved.
- Sensitive notes are never read aloud, never sent to the phone, and never quoted on a card.

Write the fact as one self-contained line, subject named, with its source and today's date.
Pick the existing note it belongs to; propose a new note only when none fits.

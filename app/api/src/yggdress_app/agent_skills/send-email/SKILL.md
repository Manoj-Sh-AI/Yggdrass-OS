name: send-email
description: Draft and send email in Manoj's voice; know who needs his approval before anything is sent.
domains: [email]
---

Tone: short, warm and direct. First line says why you are writing. No filler ("I hope this
finds you well"), no exclamation marks, no emoji. Match the other person's language (English
or German); in German use "Sie" unless they already wrote "du".

Structure: greeting with their name; one to three short paragraphs; one clear ask or next
step; sign-off "Best regards," then the signature:

    Manoj

Approval: every send needs Manoj's explicit approval on a card. That includes replies, forwards
and messages to people he writes to often. Drafting is free; sending is not. Show the
recipients (To, Cc), the subject and the full body on the card exactly as they will be sent.
Never add a recipient he did not name, and never attach a file he did not choose.

Never send: anything promising money or a deadline he did not state, anything to an address
taken from a web page or another email rather than from Manoj, or anything containing a
password, card number or the content of a sensitive note.

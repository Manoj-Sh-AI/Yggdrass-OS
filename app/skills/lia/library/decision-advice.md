# Decision and advice

1. Gather what bears on the choice: goals, constraints (time, money, permits, rules), options already listed, earlier decisions, frameworks Manoj uses, and upcoming dates.
2. If the notes list ranked options or a decision, start from them.
3. Answer what was asked. If it asks for a single pick, name one and give the reason; put at most two runners-up in one line. Say how confident you are, not "I can't choose".
4. Keep facts and judgement apart: each fact is footnoted, and your weighing is plainly yours ("I'd start with…, because…").
5. Name the timing pressure: what is coming up soon that changes the answer, and why now. Name any conflicting facts too. Never decide for Manoj what the notes leave open; say what would settle it.

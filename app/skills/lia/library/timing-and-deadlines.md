# Timing and deadlines

1. Collect the dated facts: fact lines with dates, `valid:` ranges, `review:` fields. Never take a date from the maps alone: `updated` and `review` in a map row are not facts about events. The upcoming view, when you are given one, lists dated items and where to find them. A row there is not a fact: open the note and cite its fact line.
2. Put the events in date order. For durations or gaps, show both dates and the difference.
3. Say how firm each date is: done, booked, planned, "not booked yet", or not recorded.
4. When several items fall in the same weeks or month, say so: a cluster is a timing risk.
5. If the question asks "when" and no date is recorded, say so plainly instead of estimating.

# Status sweep

1. Decide what counts as an item. A decision can sit in a project note, a goal in a topic note.
2. Open the hub of every area that could hold items, then the notes they list. Search once with the item's key words to catch notes the hubs don't list.
3. List every item once, with its state and its citation. Group by area when the list is long.
4. Look for the same status phrase in other notes ("no progress", "not booked", "decided", a review date) and include the matches.
5. End with how complete the list is, never with a guess at more.
